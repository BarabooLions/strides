About the Event

The Strides Against Diabetes 5K Run/Walk is an annual community fundraiser supporting diabetes awareness and healthy living initiatives throughout the Baraboo area. Hosted by the Baraboo Lions Club in conjunction with the SJS Sweet Corn Fest, the event welcomes runners, walkers, families, and community supporters of all ages.
